Subson - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Subson helps you keep track of subscriptions, expenses, income and savings. Your financial information is yours: we do not collect it, we cannot see it, and we never sell or share it.

What we collect

Nothing. Subson has no account system, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The developer does not run any server that receives your data.

Where your data is stored

  • On your device. Everything you enter (subscriptions, expenses, receipt photos, income, salary, loans, accounts, goals and settings) is stored in the app on your iPhone.
  • In your private iCloud (Subson Pro, optional). If iCloud sync is active, the same data is synced through your own private iCloud database so it appears on your other devices. Only you can access it with your Apple ID; the developer has no access. It uses your iCloud storage.
  • Backups and exports are files you create yourself and save or send wherever you choose.

Camera and photos

The camera and photo library are used only when you scan or pick a receipt. Text recognition runs entirely on your device using Apple's Vision framework. Images are not uploaded anywhere except your own iCloud if sync is on.

Family (Subson Family, optional)

If you create or join a family, the spending you choose to share is copied into a shared iCloud area owned by the family organizer, using Apple's iCloud sharing. Partners see only what each one allows; children's spending is visible to their parents; children can't see the parents' data. Only invited family members can access it, the developer cannot. Leaving the family removes your shared data; everything stays in your own Subson.

Public holidays

For countries without built-in holidays, the app downloads the public holiday calendar from date.nager.at. The request contains only the country and year, no personal or financial data.

Exchange rates

To convert currencies, the app downloads public exchange rates from open.er-api.com. This request contains no personal or financial data; like any internet request, the provider can see your IP address.

Notifications and Face ID

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. Face ID / passcode lock is handled by iOS; the app never receives your biometric data.

Purchases

Subson Pro is purchased through Apple's App Store. Payment is handled by Apple; the app only receives confirmation of whether Pro is active.

Children

Subson collects no data from anyone, including children. A parent can invite a child to their Subson Family; the child's spending is then shared only with the parents in the family, through iCloud.

Deleting your data

Delete items in the app, or delete the app to remove all local data. Data in your iCloud can be removed in iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Subson.
