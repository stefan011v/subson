Common questions

Do I need an account?
No. Subson works without an account. Your data stays on your iPhone and, if you turn on iCloud sync, in your own private iCloud.

How do I restore my purchase on a new iPhone?
Open Settings in Subson → Subson Pro → Restore purchases.

How do I cancel a subscription?
On your iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Subson.

How does Family work?
The organizer creates a family in Settings → Family and sends the invite link. Partners see only what each one chooses to share; parents see their children's spending. Everything is shared through iCloud - there is no Subson server.

How do I delete my data?
Delete items in the app, or delete the app. Data in iCloud can be removed in iPhone Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Subson.
